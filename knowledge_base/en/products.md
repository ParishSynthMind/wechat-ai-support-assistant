# Teas and brewing

## Shelf life and storage
Green and white teas are best within 12 months. Oolong within 18 months. Pu'er improves with age and has no expiry.
Store tea sealed, away from light, heat and strong smells. Do not keep green tea in the fridge once opened.

## Brewing green tea
Use water at 80 degrees C, about 3 grams of tea per 150 ml, and steep for 1 to 2 minutes. The leaves can be steeped 3 times.

## Caffeine
All our teas contain caffeine except the herbal blends (chrysanthemum, rooibos, mint). Green and white teas have less caffeine than black tea or coffee.
