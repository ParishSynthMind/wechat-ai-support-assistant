# Orders and delivery

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 3pm (China Standard Time) on a working day leave our Shanghai warehouse the same day.
Mainland China: 2 to 4 working days by SF Express. Hong Kong and Macau: 3 to 5 working days.
International (UK, EU, UAE, USA, Singapore, Australia): 7 to 12 working days by tracked post.

## Delivery costs
Mainland China: free on orders over 199 RMB, otherwise 12 RMB.
International: a flat 120 RMB, free on orders over 800 RMB. Import duty and local taxes are paid by the customer.

## Tracking an order
A tracking number is sent by WeChat message and email when the parcel leaves the warehouse.
Tracking can also be seen under "My orders" in our WeChat shop. If there has been no tracking update for 5 working days, a colleague will check with the courier.

## Changing or cancelling an order
An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge until it is packed, usually within 2 hours of payment.
After it has been packed it cannot be changed, but it can be returned (see Returns).

## Payment methods
We accept WeChat Pay, Alipay, and Visa or Mastercard through the website. Prices are in RMB.
