# Returns and quality

## Returns
Unopened tea can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price.
Opened tea can only be returned if there is a quality problem. Teaware can be returned within 14 days if unused and in its original box.
Return postage is paid by the customer unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
WeChat Pay refunds usually show within 1 to 3 days after that.

## Damaged or wrong items
If something arrives damaged or wrong, send a photo within 48 hours of delivery and a colleague will arrange a replacement or refund. You do not need to send the item back first.
